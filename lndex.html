LNDEX settles purely over Lightning. Both legs of every trade travel through Lightning channels: yours and the maker's, on both assets. No submarine legs, nothing on-chain, so a settled trade is instant and final. To buy an asset here you need inbound capacity in that asset; to sell, spendable capacity. Cross-chain BTC trades run over Bitcoin testnet4 Lightning the same way. Click an order to fill it.
