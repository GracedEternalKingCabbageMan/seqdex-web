Inbound liquidity is the LNDEX entry ticket. You cannot receive an asset over Lightning without inbound capacity in that asset, so a buyer on the LNDEX needs inbound capacity in the asset they are buying before the trade can settle. This page shows your per-asset channel state and sells you the capacity you are missing.

Provided just-in-time by the LSP: your wallet asks for capacity toward its own hosted node, and a channel is opened or extended to carry it. Your device co-signs everything; the LSP can route but never move your funds.
